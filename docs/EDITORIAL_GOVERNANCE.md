# JFA-11 Public editorial baseline

## Status and source

JFA-11 pages are authored in reviewed Git source, compiled into ordinary HTML by the server. The source file `apps/web/content/editorial.ts` contains introductory copy and participation paths; the page components include adjacent public explanations. **This is not yet a CMS or a live editorial approval workflow.** JFA-13/14 will introduce structured draft, peer review, publish and withdrawal controls.

## Content rules

- The site welcomes people seeking to understand any rights concern, as well as witnesses, people pressured to act, helpers, researchers, or unaffiliated visitors. It is not restricted to criminal defendants, protesters or detained people.
- Do not imply that the movement or website operates a secure testimony channel, legal clinic, FOIA filing service, volunteer roster, or public archive before those downstream services are verified.
- Do not imply that publication verifies an allegation; label account, source document, assessment, and interpretation distinctly.
- Do not fabricate statistics, participation numbers, cases or endorsements.
- No confidential information collected by these static routes. Do not add trackers, contact forms or sensitive submission URLs without privacy/security assessment.
- Each core route renders server-side HTML for low-JavaScript browsing. Preview routes can be indexed only if content is accurate; future interactive gateways need release checks.
- Git history presently establishes change provenance; the dedicated editorial audit and rollback system is later roadmap work.

## Acceptance checks

1. Public entry to stories, records and participation is reachable from home even when downstream applications are unavailable.
2. No button claims to accept a story or public-record request when its target cannot do so.
3. Content separates movement participation from political-party membership.
4. A visitor who has not been accused or arrested is explicitly included.
5. Rendered content has headings, links and meaningful text without client JavaScript.
